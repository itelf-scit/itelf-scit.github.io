SCIT’s MBA in Information Technology Business Management (ITBM) is a premier postgraduate programme that brings technology and management together. It is designed to develop techno-managerial professionals who can understand business challenges, work with emerging technologies and turn ideas into practical solutions. With a strong focus on IT, business strategy and industry relevance, the programme prepares students for leadership roles across the evolving technology landscape.
