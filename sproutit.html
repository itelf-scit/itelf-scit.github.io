Featured Event / 01

Biznovator

Innovation Seeds Tomorrow

Build from a strong idea, validate it, shape the business model and pitch it live. No prior startup experience is required — only the willingness to build.

View the 3 rounds

Participation

Eligibility Criteria

  • Open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students, as well as the general public.
  • Participants may be students or public; there is no requirement to be currently enrolled in an educational institution.
  • Teams may consist of 1 to 5 members.
  • Teams from different colleges and academic backgrounds are welcome to participate together.
  • Each participant can be part of only one team.
  • No prior startup experience is required — only a strong idea and the willingness to build.

Competition protocol

Rules & Regulations

  • Participants must present a valid government ID whenever requested.
  • All submissions must be original. Plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Teams must strictly use the official templates shared by the organizing committee.
  • Participants must not mention the name of their college, university, or any other institution in their Round 1 or Round 2 submissions.
  • No edits or resubmissions will be accepted after the submission deadline.
  • Finalist teams must attend the Grand Finale at SCIT, Pune.
  • Appropriate language, professionalism, and ethical conduct must be maintained throughout the competition.

Rounds & Point Criteria

From blank canvas to live capital-room energy.

3 progressive rounds
01

Round 01

Idea Validation Canvas

Turn the seed of an idea into a validated opportunity map.

Submission / Pitch includes

  • Problem Statement
  • Customer Segment
  • Existing Alternatives
  • Proposed Solution
  • Unique Value Proposition
  • Key Assumption
  • Financial Projection (optional)

Evaluation focus

Problem Clarity & Relevance · Customer Understanding · Solution Fit & Uniqueness · Validation & Evidence · Overall Canvas Quality

02

Round 02

Business Model + Elevator Pitch

Prove that the idea can become a business people understand and remember.

Submission / Pitch includes

  • 3–5 page Business Model Proposal
  • Executive Summary
  • Business Model Canvas
  • Go-To-Market Strategy
  • Revenue Model & Pricing
  • Competitive Landscape
  • Elevator Pitch Video

Evaluation focus

What are you building? · Who is it for? · How will you make money? · Why now? · Why your team?

03

Round 03

Grand Finale — Live Pitch Day

The top finalist teams pitch before investors, startup mentors and industry experts.

Submission / Pitch includes

  • 7 minutes — Startup Presentation
  • 5 minutes — Investors Q&A
  • Problem & Solution
  • Market Opportunity
  • Business Model
  • Revenue Strategy
  • Financial Projections
  • Product Demonstration
  • Team & Vision

Evaluation focus

Clarity · Viability · Conviction · Execution · Vision

Read before submitting

Important Guidelines

  • All submissions must follow the official templates.
  • Original work is mandatory.
  • Any plagiarism or false information will result in disqualification.
  • No college or institution name should appear in submissions before the finale.
  • Submission deadlines are strictly enforced.
  • The organizing committee's decision will be final.
